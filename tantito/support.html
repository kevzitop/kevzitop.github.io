How is “You can spend today” calculated?

We add up your accounts, subtract what's already committed until your next paycheck (recurring bills, what's due on your cards, and the cushion you don't touch), and spread the rest over the days left. On Home, tap “See how it's calculated” to see every line.

How much does a card purchase take away?

From the day of the purchase, it takes away what will show up on your next statement: the first installment. Installments for later months are shown separately.

My balance doesn't match my bank's

In Net worth, tap the account and enter the real balance. The app records an adjustment for the difference.

I deleted a transaction by mistake

In Activity, tap the trash icon. Deleted items can be recovered for 30 days.

I'm not getting payment reminders

Turn them on in Settings → Notifications and allow notifications when your phone asks. On some Android phones, also check that battery saver isn't restricting the app.

How do I add the widget to my home screen?

On Android, touch and hold an empty spot on your home screen → Widgets → Tantito, or go to Settings → “Add the widget to your home screen”. If you don't want the amounts to show, turn on Settings → Privacy → “Hide amounts in the widget”.

I'm switching phones

Your data lives only on this phone and doesn't move to the new one by itself. Before switching, export your transactions from Settings → Your data → “Export transactions (CSV)” and keep the file.

Does it need the internet? Does it connect to my bank?

No. Tantito works offline and doesn't connect to any bank: you record your transactions or import a CSV statement. That way your data never leaves your phone.

Is my data safe?

It's stored only on your phone, and you can lock the app with your fingerprint, face, or screen lock. Read the privacy policy.

Card, installment, and loan figures are estimates based on the rate and dates you enter; your statement has the actual figures. Tantito is not financial advice.
