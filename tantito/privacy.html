Tantito Privacy Policy

App version 1.0 for Android and iPhone · Last updated October 23, 2026

In short: Tantito does not collect any of your data. Everything you enter stays on your phone and is never sent to us or anyone else. There is no account, server, analytics, advertising, or third-party tool that receives information.

What the app stores and where

Your accounts, cards, loans, transactions, recurring payments, goals, budgets, and settings are stored in a database inside the app, on your phone.

  • Android: the database lives in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot access. The Android app does not have permission to use the internet.
  • iPhone: iOS protects the database with Data Protection: the file is encrypted with your passcode while the phone is locked.

Backups and switching phones

  • Android: the app excludes its data from Google cloud backups and from transfers to a new phone. If you switch phones, export your transactions first (see below).
  • iPhone: if you use iCloud or computer backups, iOS may include the app's data in those backups, under your own accounts and Apple's policies. We have no access to them.

Fingerprint, face, or passcode lock

If you turn on the lock, your phone's system (Android or iOS) verifies your identity. The app only learns whether verification succeeded; it never receives or stores your fingerprint, face, pattern, or passcode.

Reminders

If you turn on payment reminders, the app schedules local notifications on your phone. They never go through a server. On Android, the app asks for permission to show notifications and reschedules reminders when the phone restarts.

Widget

If you add the Tantito widget to your Android home screen, it shows what you can spend today to anyone who sees your screen. You can hide the amounts in Settings → Privacy → “Hide amounts in the widget”. The widget reads the data on your phone and sends nothing.

Import and export

You can import a statement in CSV format and export your transactions to a CSV file. In both cases you choose the file with the system file picker, and the app only reads or writes that file. What you do with the exported file (keep it, share it) is up to you.

Deleting your data

You can delete all your data from Settings → “Delete all my data”, or by uninstalling the app. Deleted data cannot be recovered, because no other copy exists.

Your rights

Because we do not receive or store any of your data, we hold no information about you to access, correct, or delete: you do all of that directly in the app. If you have a privacy question, email us at the address below.

Children

The app is intended for adults and is not directed at children. It does not collect data from anyone.

Changes

If a future version adds features that need to send data (for example, an account to sync between phones), this policy will be updated before that version is released, will explain what is sent and why, and the app will ask for your consent before using those features.
